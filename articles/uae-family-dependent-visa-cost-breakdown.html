UAE family/dependent visa: full cost breakdown

Sponsoring a spouse, child, or parent on a UAE residence visa involves an eligibility bar, a medical test, an Emirates ID, and stamping fees — a specific stack, not one flat "visa fee."

Sponsoring a family member's residence visa in the UAE is a defined process with a specific cost stack, most of it paid per dependent, per visa cycle (typically every 2-3 years depending on the sponsor's own visa validity). The individual line items are each modest; the total for a family of several dependents adds up faster than the headline "visa cost" figure suggests.

Sponsor eligibility: the starting gate

To sponsor a spouse or children, the sponsor generally needs a minimum monthly salary (commonly cited around AED 4,000, or AED 3,000 plus employer-provided accommodation, though this can vary by emirate and case) and suitable accommodation (an Ejari-registered tenancy in their own name, sized appropriately for the family). Sponsoring a parent typically carries a higher income threshold and additional requirements, since parent sponsorship is treated as a separate, more stringent category.

Entry permit and status change (if applicable)

If the dependent is entering the UAE for the first time on this visa, an entry permit is issued first, commonly costing somewhere in the AED 500-1,000 range, before the dependent can proceed to the in-country steps below.

Medical fitness test

Each dependent aged 18 and above must pass a medical fitness test (blood test and chest X-ray, screening mainly for communicable diseases) at an approved government health center, typically costing around AED 300-700 depending on the emirate and whether an expedited "VIP" service is used. Children under 18 are generally exempt from this requirement.

Emirates ID

Every resident, including dependents of any age, must register for an Emirates ID, with the fee scaled by the visa's validity period — commonly somewhere in the AED 100-400 range depending on whether it's issued for a 1, 2, or 3-year visa.

Residence visa stamping

The final step, visa stamping into the passport, is typically the largest single line item — commonly in the AED 1,000-1,500+ range per dependent depending on the visa's validity period (longer validity costs more upfront but is renewed less often).

Added up, sponsoring one adult dependent commonly runs somewhere in the AED 2,500-4,000 range for a fresh visa cycle, with renewals following a similar structure roughly every 2-3 years. Sponsoring several family members multiplies this per person, which is the detail that makes the total meaningfully larger than the number on any single fee schedule.

The actual question before applying

Not "what does a family visa cost" as a single figure, but "what's the full per-dependent stack for my specific family size, sponsor income bracket, and chosen visa validity period." Free zones and mainland sponsorship can carry different specific fee schedules, so confirming the exact current figures with a typing center or immigration service tied to the sponsor's specific jurisdiction is the only way to get a number to actually budget against.
