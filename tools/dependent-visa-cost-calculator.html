How this is calculated

This adds up the standard per-dependent cost stack described in our family visa cost breakdown, using typical mid-range fees for each line item:

  • Entry permit: ~AED 750 per dependent entering the UAE for the first time on this visa
  • Medical fitness test: ~AED 500 per adult dependent (18+ only — children are generally exempt)
  • Emirates ID: ~AED 150-350 per dependent, scaled to your chosen visa validity
  • Visa stamping: ~AED 1,000-1,600 per dependent, scaled to your chosen visa validity
  • Typing/service center fees: ~AED 200 per dependent

Longer visa validity costs more per dependent upfront (higher Emirates ID and stamping fees) but is renewed less often — a 3-year visa costs more today but spreads that cost over more years than a 1-year visa.

This is an estimate using typical mid-range published fees, not a quote — actual costs vary by emirate, free zone vs. mainland sponsorship, and case specifics. Confirm current figures with GDRFA, ICP, or a licensed typing center before applying.

Frequently asked questions

Do children need a medical fitness test for a UAE dependent visa?

No — medical fitness tests are generally only required for adult dependents (18+). Child dependents are typically exempt.

Why does a longer visa validity cost more upfront?

Because Emirates ID and stamping fees scale with the visa validity you choose — a 3-year visa costs more per dependent today, but you renew far less often, so the cost spreads over more years than a 1-year visa.

What's the entry permit fee for, and who needs it?

It's roughly AED 750 per dependent who is entering the UAE for the first time on this visa. It doesn't apply to dependents already living in the country.

Do these figures vary by emirate or sponsorship type?

Yes — these are estimates using typical mid-range published fees. Actual costs vary by emirate, free zone vs. mainland sponsorship, and case specifics, so confirm current figures with GDRFA, ICP, or a licensed typing center before applying.
